I am a Data & Software Engineer with over 10 years of experience turning raw, messy information into actionable insights. My passion lies in architecting end-to-end systems that bridge the gap between physical hardware signals and high-level business intelligence.

### Where I'm Working Now

Currently, I build full-stack solutions for scientific data collection at NOAA Fisheries (via T&T Consulting). My day-to-day involves taking complex NMEA sensor data from commercial fishing vessels at sea and building the AWS, PostgreSQL, and React pipelines that deliver it to the cloud in real-time.
